# IT Acceptable Use Standard

Owner: Information Technology.

## Accounts and passwords

Every person has a named account. Accounts must never be shared. Multi-factor authentication is required for all company systems. Passwords must be at least 14 characters.

## Devices

Company data may only be accessed from managed devices or through the browser with conditional access. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours so the device can be wiped.

## Generative AI tools

Approved AI tools are listed on the IT intranet page. Confidential or client data must not be pasted into AI tools that are not on the approved list.

## Software installation

Software is installed through the company portal. Requests for software that is not in the portal go to the IT service desk and are reviewed within five working days.
