# Leave Policy

Owner: People and Culture.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of annual leave per year, pro rata for part-time employees. Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next leave year. Leave requests over 10 consecutive working days need at least four weeks notice.

## Personal and carer's leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid personal and carer's leave per year. A medical certificate or statutory declaration is needed for absences of more than two consecutive days.

## Parental leave

Primary carers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave after 12 months of continuous service. Secondary carers receive 4 weeks of paid leave, which can be taken any time in the first year.

## Contractors

Contractors are not covered by this policy. Their leave terms are set out in their individual contract.
