# New Starter Onboarding Checklist

Owner: People and Culture.

## Before day one

The hiring manager raises the new starter request at least ten working days before the start date so IT can prepare a laptop and accounts.

## First week

The new starter completes the code of conduct, privacy and security training modules within the first five working days. The manager books a 30 minute check-in at the end of week one.

## Probation

Probation lasts six months. A formal review is held at three months and at six months.
