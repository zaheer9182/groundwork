# Travel and Expenses Policy

Owner: Finance. Applies to all employees and contractors on company business.

## Submitting a claim

Claims are submitted in the expenses portal with an itemised receipt for every line over 25 dollars. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted after 60 days are only paid with written approval from the Finance Director.

## Approval limits

Claims up to 500 dollars are approved by the employee's line manager. Claims between 500 and 2,500 dollars also need approval from the department head. Anything above 2,500 dollars needs Finance Director approval before it is paid.

## Flights and accommodation

Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights over six hours. Hotels should be booked through the travel desk, with a nightly cap of 220 dollars in capital cities and 160 dollars elsewhere.

## Mileage

Use of a personal car is reimbursed per kilometre at the rate published by Finance each July. Commuting between home and your usual office is not reimbursable.

## Meals

Meals while travelling overnight are reimbursed up to 75 dollars per day including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless pre-approved for a client event.
